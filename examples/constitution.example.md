# Example constitution (fictional)

> This file is a **template** showing the shape the system expects. It is not a real constitution.
> The group's real text lives in `private/constitution_v1.bn.md` (git-ignored) and is loaded into
> `constitution_versions` / `constitution_clauses` at deploy time (spec 18).
> Clause numbers here are referenced by `rules.clause_ref` and `proposal_policies.clause_ref`.

## Article 1. Purpose and term

### 1.1 Name and purpose
The fund is a private, interest-free savings group of its members.

### 1.2 Term
The fund runs from 2026-01-01 to 2028-12-31 unless dissolved earlier under clause 10.

### 1.4 Contribution
Each share contributes 5,000 BDT per month. A change needs the unanimous consent of the members.

## Article 2. Dues and fines

### 2.1 Due date
Dues are payable by the 20th of each month.

### 2.2 Late fine
A fine of 500 BDT per share is charged for a due unpaid after the 20th. Fines are charity money (3.1).

### 2.4 Suspension
After three consecutive unpaid months and a final notice, membership is suspended.

## Article 3. Money rules

### 3.1 Interest-free finance
The fund neither pays nor receives interest. Any interest received and all fines are donated to charity at year end.

## Article 4. Decisions

### 4.1 One member, one vote
Administrative decisions pass with more than half of eligible members.

### 4.2 One share, one vote
Financial decisions pass with more than half of eligible share weight.
